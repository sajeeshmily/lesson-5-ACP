Why Is Healthy Eating Important?

Eat Well, Feel Well

Healthy food gives our bodies the energy and nutrients they need to grow, learn, and stay active. A balanced diet includes different kinds of foods such as fruits, vegetables, grains, pulses, and dairy products.

Try to eat a variety of foods every day. Remember that no single food gives us everything our body needs.

Know Your Food Groups

Fruits and Vegetables

Fruits and vegetables provide vitamins, minerals, and fibre. Try to include different colours in your meals.

Grains and Protein Foods

Rice, wheat, oats, beans, lentils, eggs, nuts, and other protein foods help provide energy and support growth.

Quick Nutrition Tips

  • Drink water regularly throughout the day.
  • Eat fruits and vegetables every day.
  • Do not skip breakfast.
  • Choose fresh foods more often.
  • Enjoy treats sometimes, but do not make them your main food.
  • Eat slowly and listen to your body's hunger signals.

Tip: Small healthy choices every day can make a big difference.
